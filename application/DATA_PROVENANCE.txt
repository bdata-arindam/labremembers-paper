Experimental meaning and source locations
The main article, experimental section and Figure 2 define the response as
relative spectral absorbance loss after 18 hours of approximately one-sun
illumination in ambient air. Lower degradation means greater photostability.
It is a film photostability response, not device efficiency or photocurrent.

Supporting-information Table S1 explicitly identifies component weight fractions.
The workbook labels columns wt.-% but numerical compositions are fractions in
[0,1], summing to one. No division by 100 is applied. The robot uses equal-
concentration stock solutions; the composition variables describe component
mass fractions, not solvent volume fractions. The main article limits the
self-driving experiments to donor:acceptor ratios 1:5 through 5:1, equivalent
to total donor fraction P3HT + second donor in [1/6,5/6]. The full HTE data
include pure components and do not all satisfy that candidate restriction.
SI section S1 states merit 1 uses measured degradation, merit 2 its logarithm.
Neither is a photocurrent objective. SI Figure S4 explains the negative PCBM
result as a slight increase in absorbance; it must not be silently clipped.

Original workbook schema
Both sheets have three header rows, then 1041 HTE formulation/response rows.
System 01: [P3HT, PBQ-QF, PCBM, oIDTBR].
System 02: [P3HT, PTB7-Th, PCBM, oIDTBR].
For pandas header=None, HTE fractions are columns 0..3, response column 4.
SDA merit 1 fractions are columns 6..9, measured samples columns 10/11,
cycle markers column 12. SDA merit 2 fractions are columns 14..17, measured
samples columns 18/19, cycle markers column 20. Each SDA campaign has 60
formulations and two measured samples per formulation, across 15 batches of
four. All source values, including the two source copies of the negative
PCBM value -0.025721015222783444, remain in the tidy export.

Preparation and own data checks
extract_measured_data.py creates a separate long table; it never edits the
original workbook. Four composition fractions are checked to sum to one.
Blank cycle labels are forward-filled only within each source SDA campaign.
An Excel source row ID and formulation ID retain traceability. Data cleaning
removes header/empty rows only; no response is clipped or generated.

Each HTE sheet has 1021 unique exact composition groups. Nineteen groups have
multiple measurements, producing 20 additional records. The single group
with three observations explains why this differs from 19 extra rows.
The per-composition arithmetic means are separate model inputs; original
individual responses, counts, standard deviations, extrema and source IDs
are retained. The two systems share some observations on the zero-second-
donor face and should not be described as statistically independent studies.

Replicates and chronology
Replicate disagreement is descriptive evidence about repeated measurements,
not an empirical carryover coefficient. SDA pair RMS differences are 0.01911
(PBQ-QF) and 0.04019 (PTB7-Th); median absolute differences are 0.00618 and
0.00559. One PTB7-Th pair differs by 0.36165 and is retained. Do not silently
convert these data into an independent equal-variance Gaussian noise claim.
HTE spreadsheet ordering is not documented experimental execution order.
SDA cycle labels establish batches but do not establish within-batch order,
shared-channel use, wash commands or carryover. Neither the workbook nor
this analysis measures a physical carryover law or validates transferred
AutoFSP carryover parameters for this OPV platform.

Alternative interpolation model
opv_piecewise_affine.py builds a continuous piecewise affine interpolant of
HTE composition-group means. This is an emulator, not new measurements.
The fixed method and holdout design are recorded before fitting in
piecewise_affine_protocol.json. Fivefold composition-group validation keeps
all repeated observations of one composition together. Unsupported outside-
training-hull predictions are excluded and counted; no held-out vertices
are moved into training. The full model is fit afterwards for simulations.
It includes all pure components, so its convex hull is the whole simplex.

The model triangulates in three orthonormal simplex coordinates, recognizes
zero-volume numerical cells using exact integer-grid determinants and checks
full tetrahedron volume 1/3. It uses no jitter, response clipping, smoothing
or nearest-neighbor extrapolation. Every valid affine cell's gradient is
computed as exact rational arithmetic on the 0.02 composition grid and exact
binary64 group means, then the returned bound is rounded upward.

For L1 composition distance the cell bound is (max(g_i)-min(g_i))/2, because
composition increments sum to zero. The L2 bound is the norm of the centered
four-component gradient. Maxima over all cells bound the constructed
mathematical interpolant on its convex hull. They do not establish physical
material smoothness. Floating-point evaluations approximate that interpolant.
The very large bounds are retained rather than tuned to favor a policy.

Heldout results: 1008/1021 groups covered in each system (98.73%); 13 excluded
outside their training hulls. PBQ-QF MAE 0.02880, RMSE 0.05071, R2 0.77373.
PTB7-Th MAE 0.03714, RMSE 0.05958, R2 0.68331. Full-model L1 bounds are
65.66965 and 50.64746; L2 bounds 93.27688 and 71.94348, respectively.
These summarize an alternative model, while the separately declared cubic
Bernstein model is the primary response emulator. Validation predictions,
model arrays, source hashes and software versions are saved alongside code.
